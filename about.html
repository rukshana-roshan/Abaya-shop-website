Our Story

Dar Al Haya is a Dubai-based abaya brand dedicated to timeless elegance and modern sophistication.

We create luxury abayas in Dubai, blending refined design, quality fabrics, and thoughtful craftsmanship to offer pieces that are elegant, comfortable, and made for everyday grace as well as special occasions.

Delivering authentic Emirati haute couture modesty directly to our discerning Australian clientele in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, and nationwide.

Dubai-Made Abayas

Designed and made in Dubai, our abayas reflect the city's rich heritage and contemporary style, with a focus on premium materials, elegant detailing, and lasting quality.

Every seam is finished with French seams or concealed binding, using pure Japanese silk crepe, Saudi Nidha, and hand-applied gold bullion thread to ensure weightless drape and effortless movement.

# Dar Al Haya (دار الحياء) — Dubai-Made Luxury Abayas
*(Repository: Abaya-shop-website)*

A luxury modest fashion website handcrafted for an **Australian client** and international clientele.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

- **Brand Rebranding:** Fully transitioned from the previous template to **"Dar Al Haya"**, featuring an authentic Arabian pointed arch SVG logo and vector branding.
- **Hero Section Animatic:** A cinematic 4-scene animatic reel featuring slow Ken Burns zooms, gold shimmer headings, animated luxury seal badge, timeline progress bars, and autoplay/pause controls.
- **Australian Market Customization:**
  - Standard currency set to Australian Dollars (**AUD / A$**).
  - Australian delivery integration: Express 3–5 day delivery to **Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide**, and regional Australia with **Australia Post & DHL Express**.
  - Australian client support: Direct **WhatsApp VIP concierge** (+61 488 892 341) and Sydney client suite address (`Suite 402, 160 Castlereagh St, Sydney NSW 2000`).
  - Australian sizing chart (AU 6 to AU 20 / Lengths 52" to 62").
  - Payment icons tailored for Australian shoppers (**Afterpay, Klarna, Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Apple Pay, PayPal**).
- **Enriched Luxury Footer:**
  - Multi-column footer layout with brand story, Australian client services, private VIP newsletter, and payment & logistics trust badges.
  - Active social media integration for **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **TikTok**, **WhatsApp**, **Pinterest**, and **YouTube**.
- **Interactive Lightbox & Filtering:**
  - Full-screen high-resolution lightbox on the Gallery page.
  - Interactive collection filtering tabs on the Collections page.
  - Direct one-click WhatsApp pre-filled inquiry buttons for every abaya.

## 📁 Website Pages

1. **`index.html`** — **Home Page**
   - Hero section animatic (4 cinematic scenes with progress indicators and controls).
   - "Featured Collections" 3-card showcase (Embroidered, Classic, Occasion).
   - "New Arrivals" 4-card showcase with AUD pricing and WhatsApp inquiry.
   - Australian Delivery Information banner.
   - Comprehensive luxury footer.

2. **`about.html`** — **About Us ("Our Story")**
   - Exact layout matching template: "Our Story" text with hanging abaya boutique photo, plus "Dubai-Made Abayas" with close-up gold embroidery macro detail.
   - Brand heritage, fabric quality (pure Japanese silk crepe & Saudi Nidha), and bespoke Australian tailoring.

3. **`collections.html`** — **Our Collections**
   - Exact layout matching template (3 columns x 2 rows + 3 additional couture styles).
   - Filter tabs: *All Styles, Classic Black, Embroidered Abayas, Everyday Elegance, Occasion & Silk*.
   - Direct WhatsApp inquiry buttons with automated prefilled product messages.

4. **`gallery.html`** — **Gallery & Lookbook**
   - Exact editorial asymmetrical mosaic collage matching the template.
   - Clickable images with a custom responsive fullscreen lightbox preview.
   - "New Arrivals" 3-card row matching the template.

5. **`delivery.html`** — **Delivery Information**
   - Retouched luxury hero packaging image featuring the custom **Dar Al Haya** gold foil branding on the rigid gift box with satin ribbon.
   - Two large cards matching the template: **"Australia Delivery"** & **"International Delivery"**.
   - Australian transit times, packaging guarantee, and FAQ accordion.

6. **`contact.html`** — **Contact Us**
   - Split layout matching template: Left cream panel with WhatsApp CTA, Australian phone, Sydney and Dubai locations, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok links, plus direct messaging form; Right panel showcasing boutique rack of abayas in warm archway alcove.

7. **`shop.html`** — **Extended Catalog Showcase**
   - Complete e-commerce boutique showcase matching the 7th template design (*New Arrivals, Best Sellers, Discover More Fashion, SALE / Limited Editions, Top Picked for You*).
